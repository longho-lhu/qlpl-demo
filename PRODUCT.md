# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are lab operators, administrators, and staff managing a shared computer room. They need immediate visibility into machine availability, usage load, and maintenance conditions while supporting routine daily operations.

## Product Purpose

This product helps a computer lab or shared workstation space keep track of who is using machines, which computers are available, how long they have been used, and what requires attention. Success means users can resolve room status in seconds without browsing multiple screens or guessing at machine state.

## Positioning

The product is a practical operations dashboard for a physical computer environment. It is positioned as a clear operational tool rather than a generic analytics portal, focused on real-time administrative clarity and dependable day-to-day operations.

## Operating Context

The app is used in a university, office, or training facility where computers are shared equipment. Operators frequently check room status at the start of a session and during maintenance or support workflows. The experience should feel fast, calm, and highly scannable because it is used during quick operational decisions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- view total users and computer inventory;
- track available, in-use, and maintenance machines;
- surface usage by computer and cumulative hours;
- support user and admin roles with different access context;
- work comfortably on desktop and smaller screens without sacrificing clarity.

## Brand Commitments

The experience should feel credible, efficient, and organized. It should avoid playful gimmicks and instead favor a professional operating-system feel with strong hierarchy, controlled color meaning, and reliable information density.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository shows an internal admin dashboard for a computer management system, with Vietnamese labels, a room-based resource model, and operational status tracking. Existing UI patterns and values are the primary evidence for preserving established product meaning.

## Product Principles

- clarity over decoration;
- operational confidence over visual noise;
- consistent meaning for states and status indicators;
- efficient scanning for high-frequency admin tasks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The interface should maintain high contrast, clear labeling, readable type, and touch-friendly controls. Status distinctions must be understandable without relying on color alone, and the layout should remain legible for users who need strong hierarchy and reduced visual stress.
